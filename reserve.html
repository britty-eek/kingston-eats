Overview

Kingston Eats helps you secure a table at the city's best jerk pits, international kitchens and seafood spots. Send a request and the restaurant will follow up with you.

Whether it is a family lunch, a birthday dinner or a catch-up with friends, tell us when you would like to go and how many people are coming.

Details

This is a request, not a confirmed booking. Each restaurant decides whether it can offer your date and time.

Important to know

  • Requests can be made for today or any future date
  • Groups of 1 to 12 guests can request through this form
  • Tell us about allergies or celebrations in the special requests box
  • Please arrive on time, as tables may be released after 15 minutes
